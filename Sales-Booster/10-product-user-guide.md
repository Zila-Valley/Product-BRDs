# Product User Guide

Welcome to the Sales Booster CRM! This guide will help you navigate the system, manage your daily sales activities, and track your attendance.

## 1. What is Sales Booster CRM?
Sales Booster CRM is a platform that helps you track your prospective customers (Leads), record your daily check-ins (Attendance), communicate with your team (Chat), and log your successful sales and collections.

## 2. Who should use it?
- **Sales Executives**: To capture leads, check-in for the day, and report sales.
- **Sales Managers**: To monitor team performance, assign targets, and view attendance maps.
- **Admins**: To set up the system, manage user roles, and define business units.

## 3. Login Process
1. Navigate to the CRM URL provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Login**.
4. You will be redirected to the Dashboard.

## 4. Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of your performance. 
- You can filter the data by **Month** and **Year**.
- You will see your assigned Targets vs. Actual Sales.
- You can view your recent check-in times.

## 5. How to Add a Lead
1. From the left sidebar, click on **Leads**.
2. Click the **Add New Lead** button.
3. Fill in the prospective customer's Name, Mobile, Email, and Address.
4. Select the appropriate **Category** and **Business Unit**.
5. Save the record. The lead will now appear in your list with a "New" status.

## 6. How to Update a Lead
1. Navigate to the **Leads** list.
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon next to the lead you want to update.
3. Update their information or change their **Status** (e.g., from "New" to "In Progress").
4. Click Save.

## 7. How to Assign a Lead
*(Note: Requires Manager/Admin permissions)*
1. Edit a Lead.
2. Find the **Assigned Employee** dropdown.
3. Select the name of the sales executive who should handle this lead.
4. Save the changes. The lead will now appear in that employee's dashboard.

## 8. How to Mark Attendance (Check-in/Out)
1. Go to the **Attendance** section.
2. Click **Check-In**.
3. **Important**: Your browser/device will ask for Location and Camera permissions. You **must** allow these for a successful check-in.
4. The system will capture your GPS location and time.
5. At the end of the day, return to this screen, optionally provide an **EOD Work Update** (End of Day report), and click **Check-Out**.

## 9. How to Manage Sales & Collections
1. Once a lead is converted, go to **Sales**.
2. Click **Add Sale**.
3. Link the Sale to the respective Lead/Client and Product.
4. Enter the **Transaction Amount** and save.
5. If payment is received later, log it under the **Collections** section to offset the target.

## 10. Communication (Chat)
1. Click on **Messages** or **Communication** in the sidebar.
2. You can view **Workspaces** and **Channels** you are assigned to.
3. Type a message in the channel and hit send. You can also attach files and documents.

## 11. Common Mistakes
- **Forgetting to Check-Out**: If you forget to check-out, the background system might mark your attendance abnormally the next day.
- **Blocking Location Services**: If you block GPS on your mobile browser, you will not be able to mark attendance.
- **Duplicate Leads**: Always search the Leads list before creating a new one to avoid double-entry.

## 12. FAQ
**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
A: On the login page, click "Forgot Password" to receive a reset link via email.

**Q: Can I access this on my mobile phone?**  
A: Yes, open the CRM URL in your mobile browser (Chrome/Safari). The layout will adjust to fit your screen.
