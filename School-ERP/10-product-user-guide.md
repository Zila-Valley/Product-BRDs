# Product User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to the School ERP User Guide. This document provides quick instructions on how to use the core features of the web application.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the ERP portal URL.
2. Enter your provided Username and Password.
3. If you have forgotten your password, click "Forgot Password" to receive a reset link via email.

---

## 2. Managing Admissions

### Capturing an Inquiry
1. Go to **Front Office > Inquiries**.
2. Click **Add New Inquiry**.
3. Fill in the parent/student details and the class they are interested in.
4. Save the form. You can later convert this inquiry directly into an admission.

### Admitting a Student
1. Go to **Student Management > Admissions**.
2. Fill out the comprehensive admission form.
3. Assign the student to a Class and Section.
4. Upload necessary documents (Birth Certificate, Photos).
5. Click **Submit**. The system will auto-generate a Student ID.

---

## 3. Academics & Attendance

### Taking Class Attendance
1. (For Teachers) Navigate to **Academics > Daily Attendance**.
2. Select your Class, Section, and Date.
3. The system defaults all students to "Present". Simply uncheck the box for students who are absent.
4. Click **Save Attendance**. SMS alerts will trigger for absent students if configured.

### Creating an Exam Schedule
1. Go to **Exams > Schedules**.
2. Click **Create Schedule**, select the Exam Group (e.g., Mid-Terms).
3. Add rows for each Subject, defining the Date, Start Time, End Time, and Maximum Marks.
4. Publish the schedule to make it visible on the student mobile app.

---

## 4. Financial Management

### Collecting Fees
1. Navigate to **Fees > Collect Fees**.
2. Search for the student by Name or ID.
3. The system will display all pending dues.
4. Enter the amount being paid and the payment method (Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer).
5. Click **Collect**.
6. A receipt will be generated. You can print it or email it directly to the parent.

---

## 5. System Settings (Admin Only)

### Managing Users
1. Go to **Settings > User Management**.
2. To add a new staff member, click **Add User**, enter their details, and assign a Role (e.g., Teacher, Accountant).
3. To restrict access, go to **Settings > Roles & Permissions** and adjust the checkboxes for their specific role.
